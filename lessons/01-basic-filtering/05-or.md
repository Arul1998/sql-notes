# Lesson 5: OR

`OR` combines conditions. A row appears when at least one condition is true.

Imagine this `Employees` table:

| EmployeeId | EmployeeName | Department | Salary |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Arul | IT | 36000 |
| 2 | Priya | Finance | 45000 |
| 3 | John | IT | 30000 |
| 4 | Sara | Finance | 52000 |
| 5 | Adam | Sales | 34000 |

To display employees from IT or Sales:

```sql
SELECT EmployeeName, Department
FROM Employees
WHERE Department = 'IT'
   OR Department = 'Sales';
```

Only one of the conditions needs to be true.

SQL generally follows this pattern:

```sql
SELECT column_name
FROM table_name
WHERE condition1
   OR condition2;
```
