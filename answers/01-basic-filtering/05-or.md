# Lesson 5 Answer: OR

## Exercise

Display `EmployeeName`, `Department`, and `Salary` for employees who work in Finance or earn less than £35,000.

## Sample Table

### Employees

| EmployeeId | EmployeeName | Department | Salary |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Arul | IT | 36000 |
| 2 | Priya | Finance | 45000 |
| 3 | John | IT | 30000 |
| 4 | Sara | Finance | 52000 |
| 5 | Adam | Sales | 34000 |

## Answer

```sql
SELECT EmployeeName, Department, Salary
FROM Employees
WHERE Department = 'Finance'
   OR Salary < 35000;
```

## Expected Result

| EmployeeName | Department | Salary |
|---|---|---:|
| Priya | Finance | 45000 |
| John | IT | 30000 |
| Sara | Finance | 52000 |
| Adam | Sales | 34000 |
